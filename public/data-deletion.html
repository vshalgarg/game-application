User Data Deletion Instructions

Codemonks Games respects your right to control your personal information. If you would like to delete your account and associated personal data, you may submit a data deletion request.

This page explains how you can request the deletion of your account and personal information associated with your Codemonks Games account.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request the deletion of your account and associated data, please send an email to:

Data Deletion Email
crm@codemonks.in

Please use the following subject line in your email:

Data Deletion Request

To help us identify and process your request, please provide the following information in your email:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your name
  • The login method used to access the application, such as Google or Facebook

Providing accurate information helps us verify your account and process your deletion request efficiently.

We may request additional information to verify your identity before processing your request.

Important: For security reasons, we may need to verify that the request is being made by the account owner before deleting account data.

What Happens After Your Request

After your identity and request are verified, we will review and process your data deletion request.

The following steps may take place:

  1. Your account deletion request will be received and reviewed.
  2. Your identity and account ownership may be verified.
  3. Your account and associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized, where applicable.
  4. Information that is no longer required for legitimate business purposes will be removed in accordance with applicable requirements.
  5. Certain information may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or regulatory purposes.

We will process valid data deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe.

Please note: Once your account and associated data have been deleted, certain account information may not be recoverable.

Facebook Login Users

If you have created an account or logged in using Facebook Login, you can request the deletion of your Codemonks Games account and associated personal data by following the data deletion process described above.

Please include the email address associated with your account and mention that you used Facebook Login when submitting your request.

Google Login Users

If you have created an account or logged in using Google Login, you can request the deletion of your Codemonks Games account and associated personal data by following the data deletion process described above.

Please include the email address associated with your account and mention that you used Google Login when submitting your request.
